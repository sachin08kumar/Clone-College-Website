Admission Procedure

B.TECH (First Year)


Through Counseling

85% seats of total approved intake will be filled
through Counseling based on the CUET (PG)-2026 and
eligibility notified by AICTE/AKTU for the session 2026-27.




Procedure

  1. For participating in the counseling, the candidates are required to visit official website of the university www.aktu.ac.in regularly for detailed information which will be announced shortly.
  2. After the counseling the candidate will get an Allotment letter.
  3. Thereafter the candidate is required to visit the alloted college on the dates as mentioned in the Allotment letter for the final reporting along with the required fees and documents.
  4. Please click the below mentioned link for the detailed information of the Fee structure (Academic & hostel) and required documents. Courses Offered
  5. Before coming for the reporting to the college kindly visit the college website www.abes.ac.in to know the detailed information.



Direct Admission

15% seats will be offered to eligible candidate through
direct admissions based on the guidelines of AICTE/AKTU
and as per the college policy for the session 2026-27.

Procedure

  1. Candidate has to apply online through the college website Courses Offered
  2. Institute will shortlist the candidates on the basis of their Aggregate in qualifying exam and JEE Score/Rank.
  3. Thereafter, candidate will be called for an interview.
  4. On the basis of marks scored in the test and Interview. The candidate will be offered seat for the admission.

B.TECH (Lateral Entry)


Through Counseling

Lateral Entry to Second Year under Graduate Degree
Course shall be permissible up to a maximum of 10% of
the “Approved Intake” which shall be over and above,
supernumerary to the “Approved Intake”, plus the unfilled
vacancies of the First year. The seats will be filled
through Counseling based on the entrance exam
CUETUG-2026 and eligibility notified by AICTE/AKTU for the session 2026-27.

Procedure

  1. For participating in the written exam and counseling, the candidates are required to visit official website of the university www.aktu.ac.in regularly for detailed information which will be announced shortly.
  2. After the counseling the candidate will get an Allotment letter.
  3. Thereafter the candidate is required to visit the alloted college on the dates as mentioned in the Allotment letter for the final reporting along with the required fees and documents.
  4. Please click the below mentioned link for the detailed information of the Fee structure (Academic & hostel) and required documents. Courses Offered Before coming for the reporting to the college kindly visit the college website www.abes.ac.in to know the detailed information.
